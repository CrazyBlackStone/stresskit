    Stress calibration

    For 90 seconds, count backwards in steps of 13, as fast and accurately as you can. Type each answer and press Enter. A mistake, or taking longer than 6 s, sends you back to the start number.

    This deliberately raises your stress a little, so the monitor learns how your skin responds. Keep the hand with the straps still on the desk and type with the other hand.
